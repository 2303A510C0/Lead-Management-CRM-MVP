2 New Ideas

Extra product ideas designed to reduce lead leakage and operator decision time.

1. NEXT-ACTION AI

After every call, use stage, intent, notes, budget and timeline to recommend the single next action: call, WhatsApp, schedule tour, send match, or revive.

Why: operators waste time deciding what to do next. One recommended action makes execution faster and more consistent.

MVP: rules first; AI later. Example: “Hot + tour done + budget matched → call within 30 min.”

Success metric
↓ time-to-next-action

2. LEAKAGE GUARD

A live queue that flags leads with no owner, overdue follow-up, repeated unanswered calls, or stale movement.

Why: missed response windows directly create preventable lead loss.

MVP: SLA timer + owner alert + one-click reassign/revive.

Success metric
↓ overdue leads
