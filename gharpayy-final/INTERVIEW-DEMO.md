# Gharpayy LeadOps — 3-Minute Interview Demo

## 0: Start

Windows: double-click `START.bat`.

Or run `node backend/server.js`, then open `http://localhost:3000`.

## 1: M-POWER CALL (about 60 sec)

1. Open **Leads**.
2. Select a Hot lead.
3. Open **M-POWER CALL**.
4. Show the purpose, known context, confirmation questions, and conversation script.
5. Start the timer, stop it, select an outcome, add notes, add a follow-up date, and save.
6. Refresh and show the lead stage/next action is still updated.

**Say:** “The important part is the write-back. The call is not just a UI action; the outcome changes the CRM state through the backend.”

## 2: Movement CARE (about 60 sec)

1. Open **Movement CARE**.
2. Click **Start Today**.
3. Enter a daily promise and target.
4. Show that the system reserves up to **30 leads**.
5. Click **Take Next Lead**.
6. Select an action/result, add a note, and save.
7. Take the next lead to demonstrate one-by-one execution.
8. Click **Close Day** and show the WhatsApp-ready summary.

**Say:** “This adds an operating rhythm to the CRM: promise a result, work 30 leads, capture outcomes, and close the loop.”

## 3: Booking Flow Split (about 60 sec)

1. Open **Booking Flow Split**.
2. Show the WhatsApp-style conversation on the left and CRM workspace on the right.
3. Capture answers for the decision questions.
4. Set the next step, deadline, and closing promise.
5. Save or mark **Ready to Book**.
6. Refresh and show persistence.

**Say:** “The operator can see the customer conversation and CRM decision fields in one screen, reducing context switching and missed follow-ups.”

## 4: Two product ideas

**NEXT-ACTION AI:** recommend the single best next action after every interaction; rules first, LLM later.

**LEAKAGE GUARD:** flag unassigned, overdue, stale, and repeatedly unanswered leads so operators can revive them.

## 5: Closing line

> “I designed the MVP around a closed loop: Lead → Context → Action → Result → Write-back → Next action → Conversion.”
